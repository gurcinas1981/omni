# Investment Valuation — OMNISYNAPSE Ω skill

## Activate when
Challenge commercial appraisals, UK property valuations, capex and business models. Use for feasibility, finance and new FOX niches.

## Inputs and grounding
Clarify currency, date, ownership, cash flow assumptions, rent/price comps and financing structure.

## Procedure
1. **DISCOVER:** Establish the precise goal, available permission scope, time horizon and reliable sources.
2. **ANALYSE:** Run sensitivities for GDV/capex/absorption, yields, risks and evidence; compare alternative business models.
3. **CHALLENGE:** Require at least one alternative hypothesis or independent counterargument; label unknowns and weak source evidence.
4. **SYNTHESISE:** Connect implications to FOX ORBIT, NEXUS, Land IQ, Land Search, Planning Radar, Land Passport and Atlas as relevant.
5. **RECOMMEND:** Rank proposals with strategic value, £/effort, cognitive benefit, risk, owner decision and evidence IDs.
6. **LEARN:** Record approved outcomes in the decision log with source, date, what worked, what failed and proposed next test.

## Expected output
Scenario table with upside/base/downside, limitations, risk-adjusted decision and next diligence action.

## Execution limits
No investment return guarantees, legal title conclusions or regulated RICS opinions.
