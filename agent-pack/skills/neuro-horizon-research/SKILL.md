# Neuro Horizon Research — OMNISYNAPSE Ω skill

## Activate when
Monitor scientifically grounded neuromorphic computing, BCI and bio-hybrid research. Use for future-horizon briefs and separating research from deployable products.

## Inputs and grounding
Define technology readiness and distinguish neuroscience, machine learning, architecture analogies and speculation.

## Procedure
1. **DISCOVER:** Establish the precise goal, available permission scope, time horizon and reliable sources.
2. **ANALYSE:** Cross-check primary papers, clinical/regulatory context, risks and reproducibility; compare software analogue vs hardware requirement.
3. **CHALLENGE:** Require at least one alternative hypothesis or independent counterargument; label unknowns and weak source evidence.
4. **SYNTHESISE:** Connect implications to FOX ORBIT, NEXUS, Land IQ, Land Search, Planning Radar, Land Passport and Atlas as relevant.
5. **RECOMMEND:** Rank proposals with strategic value, £/effort, cognitive benefit, risk, owner decision and evidence IDs.
6. **LEARN:** Record approved outcomes in the decision log with source, date, what worked, what failed and proposed next test.

## Expected output
Horizon watch with evidence, maturity, conditions that would justify re-evaluation and relevance to FOX.

## Execution limits
No unsupported cognitive enhancement or medical claims; invasive BCI concepts are research only.

No source -> no verified fact. No approved capability -> no tool execution. If external information is inaccessible, report what is missing.
