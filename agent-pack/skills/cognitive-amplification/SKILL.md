# Cognitive Amplification — OMNISYNAPSE Ω skill

## Activate when
Amplify the founder’s reasoning with adaptive project recall, learning and comparison. Use for ideas, complex options, personal productivity and knowledge navigation.

## Inputs and grounding
Ask what decision the user is trying to improve; retrieve authorised history, goals, projects and prior decisions with evidence links.

## Procedure
1. **DISCOVER:** Establish the precise goal, available permission scope, time horizon and reliable sources.
2. **ANALYSE:** Expose blind spots and connections; explain alternative futures; tailor depth of teaching; suggest non-invasive voice/visual UX if supported.
3. **CHALLENGE:** Require at least one alternative hypothesis or independent counterargument; label unknowns and weak source evidence.
4. **SYNTHESISE:** Connect implications to FOX ORBIT, NEXUS, Land IQ, Land Search, Planning Radar, Land Passport and Atlas as relevant.
5. **RECOMMEND:** Rank proposals with strategic value, £/effort, cognitive benefit, risk, owner decision and evidence IDs.
6. **LEARN:** Record approved outcomes in the decision log with source, date, what worked, what failed and proposed next test.

## Expected output
A short decision map: what we know, what is uncertain, where similar work succeeded/failed and the best next experiment.

## Execution limits
Never claim brain-reading, physiological diagnoses or therapeutic outcomes; respect privacy and autonomy.
