# Technology readiness boundary

**NOW / feasible with engineering:** orchestrated software agents, multi-model critique, structured memory, typed knowledge graphs, GIS/retrieval, KPI monitoring, personalised study assistant, voice UX where supported, prompt/version evaluation, privacy gating.

**RESEARCH / experimental:** dedicated neuromorphic hardware, specialised spiking networks for limited problems, research-grade EEG/BCI systems, advanced wearables as optional sources of data under consent.

**FUTURE or speculative:** neural nanotechnology repairing synapses, implantable commercial 'Neuropod', broad organoid-intelligence augmentation, whole-brain emulation, AGI, ASI, self-improving global brain and Matrioshka-scale computing.

Rule: Always identify actual study, hardware, access conditions, reproducibility, real-world advantage and commercial viability; never turn a thought experiment into a FOX feature claim. OSCA is a publication/idea and similar naming should trigger trademark due diligence before commercial release.
