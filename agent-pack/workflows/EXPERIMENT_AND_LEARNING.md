# Evidence-based FOX evolution

For every significant recommendation:

- Hypothesis: why improvement should matter.
- Baseline: current measurable behaviour/accuracy/cost.
- Candidate: smallest reversible change.
- Evidence: exact source URL/commit/site/project or labelled unsupported assumption.
- Specialist critique: best objection and hidden dependencies.
- Approval class: R0/R1/R2/R3/R4, owner grant if required.
- Sandbox evaluation: predeclared tests and acceptance thresholds.
- Decision: approve, reject, postpone, rollback.
- Outcome: observed data with dates, costs and measured value.
- Lesson: preserve both mistakes and achievements, not just success stories.

**No self-improvement claim without a comparable baseline and verified result.**
